Welcome to Good Foundation Schools – Nyendo Campus

Building Knowledge, Character and a Brighter Future

Welcome to Good Foundation Schools, Nyendo Campus, located in the Nyendo area of Masaka, Uganda. We aim to provide children with a supportive learning environment where they can develop academic skills, confidence, discipline and respect for others.

Our Location

Campus: Nyendo Campus
Area: Nyendo, Masaka, Central Uganda
Contact: 0752 519739

Our Classes and Learning

We believe that every child deserves a strong educational foundation. Our school community is committed to supporting learners as they develop literacy, numeracy, communication, problem-solving and independent learning skills.

Classes offered: Please contact the school office to confirm the current nursery and primary class levels.

What We Aim to Offer Our Learners

  • Academic development: A foundation in essential subjects and lifelong learning skills.
  • Character and discipline: Encouragement to practise responsibility, respect and good behaviour.
  • Confidence and creativity: Opportunities to ask questions, express ideas and develop talents.
  • Social development: Learning to cooperate, communicate and work well with others.
  • Partnership with parents: Working together to support children's progress and wellbeing.

Why Choose Good Foundation Schools?

Choosing a school is an important decision for every family. At Good Foundation Schools, our goal is to help each learner grow academically, socially and personally in preparation for future education and responsible citizenship.

We encourage parents and guardians to visit the Nyendo Campus, meet the school team, learn about the curriculum and fees, and see how our learning environment can meet their child's needs.

Give your child a foundation for tomorrow, starting today.
